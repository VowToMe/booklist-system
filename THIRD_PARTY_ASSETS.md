# Third-party assets

The interface uses the following Wikimedia Commons images:

- `paper-grain.webp`
  - Source: [Free dark vintage paper page texture for layers](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Free_dark_vintage_paper_page_texture_for_layers_(2982207584).jpg)
  - Creator: Pink Sherbet Photography
  - License: [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/)
  - Processing: cropped, tone-mapped, resized, and converted to WebP.

- `detail-paper.webp`
  - Derived from the image above.
  - Processing: cropped and processed into a muted paper texture, then converted to WebP.
